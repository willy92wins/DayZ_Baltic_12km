# EV93 selected-bank checkpoint scope

This checkpoint captures the closed EV93 compound selection, its global consumer and selector evidence, crest policy, and selected-bank build evidence. It includes the fresh EV93 server-only native readback receipts, independent provenance review, and copied runtime snapshot. Large RPT files and binary artifacts are represented by path, size, and SHA-256 references.

The active `global-operator-prototype` real-sector pilot is excluded because it has independent writers and will be checkpointed separately. This checkpoint does not wait for that solver or copy its evolving files. The native readback verifies physical values at native vertices; it does not claim visual/render, client, or gameplay PASS.

The build-selected history preserves both the prior native-verifier pin failure and the NaN-metadata stage failure, along with their source/log evidence. The checkpoint script fails closed if either preserved record or the fresh native receipts drift or disappear.

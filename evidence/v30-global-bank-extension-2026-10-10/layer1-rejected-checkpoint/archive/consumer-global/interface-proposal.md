## EV91 gates: global semantics versus pilot-local gates

The following EV91 gates generalize to a combined candidate, provided they are evaluated once against the complete changed set and retain their exact predicates:

- Reconstruct the candidate from the complete window ledger and independently derived per-window detail. Every changed float32 vertex must belong to the union of detail-declared mutable nodes; each rejected window's nodes remain bit-exact B. Validate physical inward bounds and crossing-derived bounds from the pinned domain details. Do not replace these details with an EV87 radius selector.
- For every affected native support cell, `topology_check` preserves the existing signs and exact zero/nonzero classification on known P, fully finite F triangles, and the nominal SEA layer; each original strict crossing remains valid and its displacement is at most 1 m (`verify_ev88q.py:158–190`). This gate is about existing signs/crossings, not a half-sign rule.
- Keep EV91's high-field-cell protection exact at the reconstructed scope (`verify_ev91.py:638–647`), together with per-window guard/freeze sets supplied by the details. Do not assume the EV91 pilot's particular 43-node collar guard or 85-node domain globally.
- Preserve corridor contract at its actual scopes: all 19 corridor polygons are geometrically covered within `1e-5 m²`; known-P triangles touched by changed vertices have nominal minimum depth at least `0.95 m`; unknown-P corridor area and its triangle-corner heights remain exactly B (`verify_ev91.py:753–787,803–815`; `verify_ev91.py:803–815` defines the separate high-bed rule). High bed is `cls==3 && level>=2` outside the frozen 620 m Narva exclusion and must remain at least 1 m. Report the full 19-corridor minimum/unknown area, but do not silently turn the affected-triangle depth gate into a new all-map depth gate.
- Recompute compiled-face coverage and exposure over the union of actual changed-support cells plus changed unknown-F cells, using both B and H. Keep EV90's exact support-hole totals `<1e-7 m²` on both B/H, P/F band comparisons (`1e-5 m` physical, `2e-5 m` visible), and missing/growth residual outside those bands bounded by the original `0.002 m²` total and per-cell gates. Keep per-face new/worsened excess checks; a correct face cannot excuse another face above its field. Partial-F changes stay inconclusive/rejected; all-NaN F changes are resolved only if every intersecting compiled face is absent or buried by `>2e-5 m` at all intersection vertices in both B and H. WRP instance identity, model names, XZ, rotation, bounds, PBO and all source hashes remain pinned (`verify_ev91.py:676–750`; coverage helper lines 93–94, 104–164, 166–198, 219–289, 301–328).
- Recompute EV91 global terrain-family gates once over the composite H: slopes satisfy `H <= max(B,35°)+1e-4`; there are zero newly over-30° hinges on the full map (the helper computes that mask globally, despite the result key saying “touched”); every 64-cell sector touched by changed support has a maximum non-worse within `1e-4°` (`verify_ev88q.py:193–220`). Preserve bank/interior population p99, above-30 count, and both B-bracket count predicates (`verify_ev88q.py:223–242`). Report the limited population scope: it excludes 65,955 eligible wet-support nodes (2.21%), so this is not whole-map or all-eligible-node p99.

EV91's focus hinge, stencil energy, collar and D8 conditions are local requirements in the new first-layer contract, not global aggregate gates. Validate them independently for each accepted window using that entry's `center_rc/window_rc/core_rc`, detail and reconstructed stencils; apply `shape_contract.evaluate_shape` with that same center/window. The plan fixes the per-window tests (focus prominence on both axes, local maxima/sum, no new 8-neighbor minimum or constant 2×2, ≥25% stencil energy reduction, focus incident hinge reduced ≥2°, collar unchanged, local slope/hinge tests). Never reuse EV91's single `(840,370)` focus, its 85/43/42-node counts, or a radius-based selector. Global gates remain separate and are recomputed on composite H.

## Face index use and ledger boundary

Load and pin one COMPLETE EV93 index run. Treat `cell_range_rc` as a conservative bbox only: intersect each selected face with the exact target cell and NW-SE native triangle, then use the frozen EV90 affine exposure/coverage rules. A candidate face in the bbox is not evidence that it intersects or is exposed. The index is for avoiding repeated ODOL traversal; it does not replace the per-face excess scan or unknown-face certificates.

For a global batch, derive the changed vertices from the supplied ledger/detail, form the exact affected support-cell union once, and process compiled faces once for that union (or in deterministic non-overlapping chunks whose union is proven complete). Do not perform an 883k-face scan for each window. Do not define the mutable domain from the padded exposure-cell set. The EV90 cell-support expansion is only an evaluation domain for compiled geometry, not permission to change those vertices.

The author has defined `prepared.json`, ordered queue entries, immutable per-window results, append-only `ledger.jsonl`, and a final receipt. It distinguishes terminal geometric/preflight rejection and `not_authored` on non-READY rows (all preserve B) from a READY row without a terminal result, execution timeout/error, or marker without result (incomplete). A complete terminal ledger may be evaluated diagnostically, but timeout/error/incomplete state can never yield acceptance. The consumer must independently reconstruct the allowed union and validate candidate deltas against accepted detail nodes; it rejects omissions, duplicates, unknown entries, and any changed vertex outside accepted mutable nodes. For each accepted window verify direction, scalar interpolation and final float32 reconstruction from the immutable result artifact. No fabricated EV87 receipt and no dependency on author/solver code.

## Scope and remaining limits

This design is offline static geometry. Even a passing composite consumer would not establish dynamic waves, shader behavior, runtime rendering, or artistic acceptance. It also cannot certify the 65,955 omitted wet-support nodes through the existing bank/interior p99 gate; that limitation must stay explicit unless a separately approved contract adds a population.

No adapter code is written pending the exact ledger schema and final pins from root.
